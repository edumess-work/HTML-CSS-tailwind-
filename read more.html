Why Clean Water Matters

Clean water helps people stay healthy and prevents many diseases. It is also important for farming, schools, hospitals and businesses.

Ways We Use Water

  • Drinking
  • Washing
  • Bathing
  • Cooking
  • Farming

How we can save water

  1. Turn of Water when not in use
  2. Dont waste Water While Washing
  3. Use only the amount of water you need
  4. Repair leaking Pipes
